What it is

To Me is a gentle wall. Whatever you want to say, just write it down. It listens quietly and keeps it for you.

  • Just open and write: a single line is an entry too.
  • Look back on the days you've written, month by month, and search them.
  • No account, no ads. Your entries stay on your phone.
  • Members can sync entries through their own iCloud (iPhone) or Google Drive (Android), export them, and lock the diary.

这是什么

To Me 是一面温柔的墙壁。想说的话，写下来就好。它安静地倾听，为你记录。

  • 打开就写：一句话也是一篇日记。
  • 按月份翻看写过的日子，也可以搜索。
  • 没有账号，也没有广告。日记保存在你的手机上。
  • 会员可以通过自己的 iCloud（iPhone）或 Google Drive（安卓）同步日记，还能导出日记、开启日记锁。
