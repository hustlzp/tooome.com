To Me for Android Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 10, 2026

To Me is a private diary app from TinyWish Studio. This policy covers the Android app only. In short: your diary stays with you, and we don't collect your data.

What We Don't Do

  • We don't collect, receive, or store your diary entries. We don't run any server that receives them.
  • There is no To Me account or sign-in.
  • There are no ads, analytics, or third-party tracking tools.
  • The app connects to the internet only to handle membership purchases and, if you turn it on, to sync with your own Google Drive. Both are described below.

What Is Stored on Your Phone

Your entries, your unfinished draft, and your settings (such as the reminder time) are stored in the app's private storage on your phone.

Entries you delete stay in Recently Deleted for 30 days and are then permanently erased. Uninstalling the app deletes everything stored on the phone. If you turned on Google Drive Sync, the copy in your Google Drive stays until you delete it.

Google Drive Sync

Google Drive Sync is a membership feature and is off by default. It only starts when you turn it on in Settings.

  • What the app can access: when you turn it on, you choose a Google Account and allow To Me to use its own app data folder in your Google Drive. This folder is hidden from your Drive and can only be read and written by To Me. To Me can't see or access any of your other Drive files.
  • What is stored there: your entries, including their text, the times they were written and edited, and whether they were deleted. Nothing else.
  • How it's sent: data goes directly between your phone and Google over an encrypted connection. It never passes through our servers, and we can't read it.
  • Your account: the app reads your Google Account's email address to know which account is connected. It's kept only on your phone and is never sent to us.
  • Turning it off: syncing stops and the app's access to your Google Drive is revoked. Your entries on the phone and in Google Drive are both kept. To delete the copy in Google Drive, open Google Drive's settings, go to Manage apps, find To Me, and choose Delete hidden app data.

To Me's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. This information is used only to sync your entries, and is not used for ads, sold, or shared with anyone.

Membership Purchases

Purchases are made through Google Play, and Google handles your payment. We never see your payment details.

To check whether you're a member, the app uses RevenueCat, a subscription service. It sends RevenueCat a random ID created by the app, your To Me purchase records from Google Play, and basic technical information such as the app version, Android version, and language. We also use these records to see aggregated purchase statistics, such as how many people are members. It doesn't send your diary entries, your name, or your email address. RevenueCat's privacy policy applies to this data: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy/

Android Backup

If backup is turned on for your device, Android may include this app's data in the backup saved to your Google Account, so it can be restored on a new phone or after reinstalling. Google runs this service, and we can't access these backups. You can turn it off in your phone's settings. Whether a backup is end-to-end encrypted depends on your device, for example whether a screen lock is set, and we can't guarantee that a backup exists.

Things That Happen Only When You Choose

  • Share Card: when you share a card, the image goes to the app you pick. When you save it, it goes to your photo library.
  • Feedback: the Feedback option opens your email app. If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you write, and use them only to reply to you.
  • Links: Rate Us, Follow the Author, and similar options open Google Play or other apps and websites, which follow their own privacy policies.

Permissions

  • Internet: used for membership purchases and Google Drive Sync, as described above.
  • Notifications: requested only if you turn on the writing reminder. The reminder is generated on your phone.
  • Storage (Android 9 only): requested only when you save a share card to your photo library.
  • Background work: Android's scheduling components use a few system permissions, such as starting after a reboot and checking network status, to keep the reminder on time and to sync when a network is available.

Google Play

Google Play handles downloads and updates and may provide us with aggregated statistics and crash reports, such as install counts and technical crash details. Google's privacy policy applies to this data: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Children

To Me isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and its effective date.

To Me 安卓版隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 11 月 10 日

To Me 是 TinyWish Studio 开发的私密日记 App。本政策只适用于安卓版。简单来说：日记留在你这里，我们不收集你的数据。

我们不做的事

  • 不收集、不接收、不存储你的日记。我们没有任何接收日记的服务器。
  • 没有 To Me 账号，也不用登录。
  • 没有广告、统计分析或第三方追踪工具。
  • App 只在处理会员购买，以及你开启同步后与你自己的 Google Drive 同步时联网。这两项在下面分别说明。

手机上存储的内容

你的日记、未写完的草稿和设置（比如提醒时间）都存储在手机上 App 的私有空间里。

删除的日记会在“最近删除”里保留 30 天，然后被彻底清除。卸载 App 会删除手机上存储的全部内容。如果你开启过 Google Drive 数据同步，Google Drive 里的那份会一直保留，直到你删除它。

Google Drive 数据同步

Google Drive 数据同步是会员功能，默认关闭，只有你在设置里打开后才会开始。

  • App 能访问什么：打开时，你选择一个 Google 账号，并允许 To Me 使用它在你 Google Drive 里的专属目录。这个目录在 Drive 里不显示，只有 To Me 能读写。To Me 看不到、也访问不了你在 Drive 里的其他任何文件。
  • 存了什么：你的日记，包括正文、写下和修改的时间，以及是否已删除。没有其他内容。
  • 怎么传输：数据通过加密连接，直接在你的手机和 Google 之间传输，不经过我们的服务器，我们也无法读取。
  • 你的账号：App 会读取 Google 账号的邮箱地址，用来知道连接的是哪个账号。它只保存在你的手机上，不会发送给我们。
  • 关闭时：同步停止，App 对你 Google Drive 的访问权限会被撤销。手机上和 Google Drive 里的日记都会保留。要删除 Google Drive 里的那份，打开 Google Drive 的设置，进入“管理应用”，找到 To Me，选择“删除隐藏的应用数据”。

To Me 对通过 Google API 获得的信息的使用和传输，遵守 Google API 服务用户数据政策，包括其中的“有限使用”要求。这些信息只用于同步你的日记，不会用于广告、出售或分享给任何人。

会员购买

购买通过 Google Play 完成，付款由 Google 处理，我们看不到你的支付信息。

为了确认你是否是会员，App 使用订阅服务 RevenueCat。App 会向它发送一个由 App 随机生成的 ID、你在 Google Play 上购买 To Me 会员的记录，以及 App 版本、安卓版本、语言等基本技术信息。这些记录也用于查看汇总的购买统计，比如会员人数。不会发送你的日记、姓名或邮箱。这些数据适用 RevenueCat 的隐私政策：https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy/

安卓系统备份

如果设备开启了备份，安卓系统可能会把这个 App 的数据备份到你的 Google 账号，以便在新手机上或重新安装后恢复。这项服务由 Google 运营，我们无法访问这些备份。你可以在手机设置里关闭。备份是否端到端加密取决于你的设备，比如是否设置了锁屏，我们也不能保证备份一定存在。

只有你主动操作时才会发生的事

  • 分享卡片：分享时，图片会发送到你选择的 App；保存时，图片会存到你的相册。
  • 意见反馈：这个选项会打开你的邮件 App。如果你给我们发邮件，我们会收到你的邮箱地址和邮件内容，只用来回复你。
  • 外部链接：“好评鼓励”“关注作者”等选项会打开 Google Play 或其他 App、网站，它们适用各自的隐私政策。

权限

  • 网络：用于会员购买和 Google Drive 数据同步，见上文。
  • 通知：只有你打开写日记提醒时才会申请。提醒在手机上生成。
  • 存储（仅 Android 9）：只有你把分享卡片保存到相册时才会申请。
  • 后台任务：安卓的任务调度组件会用到几项系统权限，比如开机后启动、检查网络状态，以便按时提醒，以及在有网络时同步。

Google Play

下载和更新由 Google Play 处理。Google Play 可能向我们提供汇总统计和崩溃报告，比如安装量和崩溃的技术细节。这些数据适用 Google 的隐私政策：https://policies.google.com/privacy

儿童

To Me 不面向 13 岁以下的儿童，我们也不会有意收集任何人（包括儿童）的个人信息。

政策变更

如果本政策有变化，我们会更新此页面和生效日期。
